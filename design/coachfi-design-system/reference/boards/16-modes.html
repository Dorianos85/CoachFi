How the modes work

Shared everywhereMila (orange, blocky, cap), Nunito, ink-outlined buttons, pastel area colours, one strong action per screen, captions for every voice reply.
Who picks the modeKids profiles are created by a parent (nickname + age band). Teen and Adult are set at sign-up from date of birth, never shown in the app.
What differsKids: learning pet, stories, no open AI chat, no notifications. Teen: Mila with topic limits, first paycheck, online shopping. Adult: full coach.
