Fair-play rules
  • Your pet never gets sick, never dies and is never sad because of you
  • When you’re away it rests and looks forward to seeing you
  • Nothing can be bought with real money
  • After about 15 minutes your pet suggests a break
  • Scarves and backpacks are earned only by learning
For parents: “Stash it” sends a request to your app. The coin goes into the jar only after you confirm.
