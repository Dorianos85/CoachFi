Accessibility is the product, not a mode.

Target WCAG 2.2 AA minimum. Design-time review below; must be confirmed with automated checks and blind, low-vision and older participants (board 15).

2.4.11 Focus not obscuredSticky nav and sheets never cover the focused item; scroll-padding set.
2.5.7 DraggingEvery slider also takes typed values or +/− buttons.
2.5.8 Target sizeMinimum 24 px required; CoachFI uses 44 px, 56–60 px in Kids.
3.2.6 Consistent helpMila and Help always in the same place on every screen.
3.3.7 Redundant entryGoals entered once, reused in health check and plans.
3.3.8 Accessible authPasskeys / email link; paste allowed; no puzzles.
