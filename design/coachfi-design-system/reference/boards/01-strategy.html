The brand idea

A squirrel saves for winter. Mila helps you do the same — out loud.

The logo character is the product. Mila is a voice AI agent for learning: you talk, she explains money in plain words, and every conversation ends with one small step you can take today.

First user

Maya, 24 · first job · $0 saved

Paycheck to paycheck, a student loan, a first credit card. Hates spreadsheets, listens to podcasts on the bus. Wants to feel less anxious about money in two minutes a day.

US young adults 21–30 · found through short video

Positioning

For young Americans on their first paychecks who feel anxious about money, CoachFI is the voice-first AI coach — Mila, a friendly squirrel — that teaches money in two-minute conversations and turns each one into a small daily step. Unlike budgeting apps that only track, or courses that only lecture.

Decisions

Launch
  • Adult mode, US market, English
  • Free — grow the community first
  • Voice-first Mila + text fallback
  • Education and habits only
  • Course certificates as Solana NFTs
Later
  • Teen (vaporwave) · Kids (learning pet)
  • Polish and more languages
  • Plus subscription
  • Offers tab — always “Sponsored”
Never
  • Mila picking investments or products
  • Crypto tips or trading, ads, data selling
  • Streak guilt or countdown pressure
  • Voice recordings kept without consent

Risks we design around

  • “Too cute for my money.” Adult mode keeps Mila small on screen, calm voice, no bouncing — the character earns trust by being useful.
  • AI honesty. Mila is a squirrel, not a person: “AI coach, not a financial advisor” on every conversation, and “Why this?” on every suggestion.
  • Voice privacy. Audio processed and discarded by default; transcripts are the user’s, deletable any time.
  • US regulation. Education only at launch; Offers later need FTC labelling and US fintech counsel. Trademark clearance before filing.
