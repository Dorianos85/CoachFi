Rules

Construction: voxel blocks, oblique view, sharp edges; squirrel orange #E8772E, tail #C85A1E, belly #FCE7C8, cap #2B2840 with gold tassel. Animation: talking = 2 mouth frames switched by audio level (6–8 fps); listening = soft pulse on the headphones; never spinning, never bouncing in Adult mode. Reduced motion: static expression + live captions + status text (“Mila is listening…”). Never: a human face, a realistic squirrel, a different colour fur, or Mila implying she is a person or a licensed advisor.
