Healthy retention: people return because their money got better.

The goal is not screen time. Every loop below ends in a real-world action or real understanding — and none of them punishes a missed day.

North-star metric Weekly progressors Share of active users who complete ≥ 1 real financial action or lesson in a week. Time-in-app is tracked only as a guardrail — rising time with falling actions is a warning, not a win.

Notification budget

  • Max 1 push per day, 3 per week, across all features
  • Daily reminders opt-in; weekly review suggested once, at a time the user picks
  • Quiet hours 21:00–08:00 by default
  • 3 ignored in a row → frequency halves automatically and we tell the user
  • Every notification carries value in itself (“$64 under your usual this week”)

Copy rules

“Welcome back — your next step is ready.” “You broke your 12-day streak!” “Quiet month? That happens. Want a lighter plan?” “Only 2 hours left to claim your reward!”

Removed on purpose

  • Variable-ratio rewards — the slot-machine schedule in the current plan; replaced by predictable rewards for real actions
  • Streak loss & paid streak freezes
  • Countdown timers & artificial scarcity
  • Money leaderboards — social comparison about money harms the people we most want to help
  • Rewards with cash value — NFT certificates stay: proof of a finished course, earned only by learning, never bought or sold in the app
