Simulated walkthroughs · six profiles

Financially inexperienced adult Worked: no income questions; one step at a time felt doable. Friction: “safety net” vs “emergency fund” used interchangeably — confusing. Fix: one term in UI (“safety net”), the other in a tap-to-explain glossary.
Busy parent Worked: 2-minute step; shared family goal. Friction: Sunday review collides with family time; “I did it” assumes they can transfer right now. Fix: user-chosen review time; add “Remind me tonight” to the step.
Teenager (13–17) Worked: short lessons, visible path. Friction: falls between Kids (6–12) and adult — adult screen feels like “my parents’ app”. Gap: design a Teen tier (first pay, phone plans, online shopping) with brighter tints.
Blind screen-reader user Worked: voice-first Mila; quiz uses real radio groups. Friction: live region would read partial voice transcripts aloud while Mila listens. Fix: announce only final replies; transcript region aria-live="off" during capture.
Low-vision user Worked: rounded Nunito body face with tabular figures; bands not colour-dependent. Friction: at 200% the next-step buttons fall below the fold on Today. Fix: at large text, put the action above the explanation in the card.
Older adult, new to finance apps Worked: simplified mode; no jargon in onboarding. Friction: unsure whether to trust an “AI coach” with money questions; wants a person. Fix: visible “Contact a person” route in Help [SUPPORT CHANNEL]; explain what Mila can’t do.

A/B hypotheses

  1. Check-in before sign-up raises activation vs account-first.
  2. Health bands raise “make a plan” clicks vs a 0–100 score, without lowering trust ratings.
  3. Opt-in reminders: lower D7, but higher D30 and fewer notification opt-outs than opt-out reminders.
  4. Showing “Why this?” inline raises Mila suggestion acceptance.

Metrics we will measure (none measured yet)

  • Activation — finished check-in + saw a next step, day 0
  • First lesson completion — within 7 days
  • First useful financial action — step marked done within 7 days
  • D7 / D30 retention — returned and completed ≥ 1 action
  • Repeat learning — lessons in ≥ 3 of first 4 weeks
  • A11y task completion — core tasks with assistive tech
  • Financial confidence — self-report at day 0, 30, 90 (candidate: CFPB 5-item Financial Well-Being Scale, validated in Polish first)
