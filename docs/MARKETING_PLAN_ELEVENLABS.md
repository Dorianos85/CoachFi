# Coach FI - ElevenLabs Marketing Plan

Date: 2026-06-09

## 1. Positioning

Coach FI should be positioned as:

**A voice-first family financial health coach for people who earn money but do not yet have a calm system.**

Short public pitch:

> Coach FI starts with a free Financial Health Check. Families unlock a personal score, then Mila turns it into daily actions: spend consciously, save automatically, learn with children and build long-term habits.

Do not market Coach FI as a trading app. Use Dorian's trading audience as a distribution channel, but position Coach FI as the foundation people need before they trade:

- saving habit,
- emergency fund,
- inflation awareness,
- risk and behavior,
- compound interest,
- no leverage-first messaging.

Primary audience:

- middle-class and upper-middle-class families,
- wealthy households with lifestyle spending leaks,
- professionals and entrepreneurs with income but no repeatable financial system,
- parents who want to teach children money habits.

The public message should not lead with debt rescue. Crisis support remains a hidden/free safety path triggered only by low-score answers or explicit help intent.

## 3. Core Funnels

### Funnel A - Free Financial Health Diagnosis

Audience: families, middle-class earners, professionals, entrepreneurs.

Path:

1. Short video: "You earn money, but does your family have a system?"
2. CTA: "Check your Financial Health Score for free."
3. User lands on `/health-check`.
4. User gets a preview insight without account.
5. To unlock score and plan, user logs in and accepts Terms/Privacy.
6. Mila reads the score and recommends a 30-day family plan.
7. User starts a 7-day Mila Voice Coach trial.

Primary CTA:

> Zrób darmową diagnozę zdrowia finansowego rodziny.

### Funnel B - Premium Voice Coach Trial

Audience: users who completed the score.

Path:

1. Score page shows top money leak.
2. Mila reads one personalized insight.
3. Trial offer: "Let Mila guide your family for 7 days."
4. Trial limits are clear: session count, voice minutes/credits, renewal date, cancellation.
5. Premium Family offer after first completed 3-day streak.

Primary CTA:

> Przetestuj głosowego coacha przez 7 dni.

### Funnel C - Parents / Kids

Audience: parents who want to teach children money basics.

Path:

1. Short video: "How to explain needs vs wants to a child."
2. CTA: "Try Kids Mode."
3. User lands on `/kids`.
4. Child gets stars, not tokens.
5. Parent sees safe educational framing.

Primary CTA:

> Naucz dziecko pieniędzy bez stresu i bez tradingu.

### Funnel D - Partners

Audience: schools, universities, banks, brokers, family offices, premium communities.

Path:

1. LinkedIn / email demo clip.
2. CTA: "See voice-first family financial education demo."
3. Partner lands on `/partner` or a dedicated deck.
4. Mila voice shows differentiation.
5. Follow-up with dashboard + white-label offer.

Primary CTA:

> Give your users financial education they actually finish.

## 5. Weekly Production System

### Monday - Script Batch

Create 10 scripts:

- 4 Financial Health Score clips,
- 2 Before You Trade clips,
- 2 Mila explainers,
- 1 kids lesson,
- 1 partner/accessibility clip.

Script length:

- Shorts/Reels/TikTok: 20-45 seconds.
- YouTube long-form insert: 60-120 seconds.

### Tuesday - Voice Production

Use ElevenLabs:

- one consistent Mila voice,
- one host/Dorian-style voice only if rights and consent are clear,
- generate PL first,
- create EN/ES/PT-BR variants only for best-performing clips.

### Wednesday - Edit

Create:

- 5 Shorts/Reels/TikToks,
- 1 YouTube community post,
- 1 LinkedIn clip,
- 1 app demo GIF/video.

Always include captions and UTM links.

### Thursday - Publish and Test

Publish 2-3 clips.

Track:

- hook retention after 3 seconds,
- average watch time,
- CTA click rate,
- health-check completion,
- account/terms unlock,
- trial activation.

### Friday - Optimize

Double down on the best hook:

- re-record voice with different tone,
- test faster/slower delivery,
- test "Mila says" vs "Financial Health Score" title,
- cut any message that sounds like financial advice or trading promise.

## 9. Safety and Trust Rules

Always say:

- educational only,
- no investment recommendations,
- no promised returns,
- no leverage/futures/speculation education,
- Kids Mode has no wallet, no public leaderboard and no free-form financial advice.

For voice:

- disclose AI-generated or synthetic voice where needed,
- do not clone any person's voice without clear permission,
- do not use a voice to imply endorsement from banks, influencers or public figures,
- keep scripts short to control credits.

For trials and subscriptions:

- show price and renewal date before payment,
- no prechecked marketing consent,
- no hidden cancellation route,
- no fake countdowns or guilt-copy,
- no automatic material price change without informed consumer consent.
