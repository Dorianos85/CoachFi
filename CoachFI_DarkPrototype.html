What is compound interest and why does it matter?
Compound interest means earning interest on your interest — not just on the original amount. It's the most powerful force in personal finance, and it works both for and against you.

Example: 1 000 PLN at 7% annual return:
· After 10 years → 1 967 PLN
· After 20 years → 3 870 PLN
· After 30 years → 7 612 PLN

The same 1 000 PLN at simple interest would only give you 1 700 PLN after 10 years. The extra 267 PLN — that's compound interest working for you.

Why it matters for Poland: Only ~33% of Poles correctly understand compound interest (OECD 2020). This single gap explains why many people underestimate how fast high-interest debt grows — and why so few join PPK pension schemes early enough.
📊 OECD/INFE 2020 · Poland
